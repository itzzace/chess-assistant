Chess Assistant

On-device move suggestions for Chess.com — best moves from Stockfish, or human-like moves from the Maia 3 neural engine, drawn right on the board.

+ Add to Sileo Download .deb

Requires a jailbroken device (rootless). Sideload IPA builds available on the releases page.

What it does

♞ Two engines

Bundled Stockfish 16 for the strongest move, or Maia 3 for the move a human at your rating would actually play.

🎯 ELO-tuned

Set strength from 400 to 3500. Maia conditions its predictions on the ELO you choose.

➞ On-board arrows

Suggestions drawn as arrows with eval or win-probability labels. Show 1–3 candidate moves at once.

📈 Move analysis

Chess.com-style grading — Brilliant to Blunder — with per-game accuracy tracking.

📱 Everywhere

Works in online games, bots, coach, daily puzzles, and the puzzle trainer.

🔒 Fully offline

Everything runs on-device with CoreML. No network, no accounts, no data leaves your phone.

Install

1
Add the repo

Tap Add to Sileo above, or add this URL manually in Sileo → Sources.

https://itzzace.github.io/chess-assistant/
2
Install Chess Assistant

Find it in the repo and install, then respring.

3
Open Chess.com

Tap the floating ♞ button → settings. Toggle Maia for human-like play, set your ELO, and start a game.
